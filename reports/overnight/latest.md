## Experiments / Results

All figures below are read from committed files at origin/hilarius. Underlying `cv_results_*.json` files are not committed (see Potential Issues 1), so these are derived CSV values.

- **ESM-C-300M pad-token k-sweep** (`runs/pca_sweep_delong/pca_sweep_results.csv`): pooled-OOF AUC by k — 96 (50% EV) 0.6442, 2278 (80%) 0.6973, 3663 (85%) 0.6943, 5883 (90%, anchor) 0.6885, 9854 (95%) 0.6835. k=2278 beats the anchor by Δ=+0.0087 (paired DeLong z=6.70, p=2.0e-11, Holm p_adj=8.1e-11, `pca_sweep_delong.csv`); the script's optimum-PC rule (smallest k statistically indistinguishable from best, Holm) flags k=2278. On the informative padded subset, k=2278 peaks at 0.7055, but its margin over the anchor (Δ=+0.0145, p=0.036) is ns after Holm (p_adj=0.108).
- **ESM-IF eos/bos-repeat k-sweep** (same CSVs): AUC decreases monotonically over the ladder — 345 (50% EV) 0.6411, 2125 (80%) 0.6402, 2974 (85%) 0.6374, 4308 (90%, external anchor) 0.6328, 6700 (95%) 0.6267, 11243 (99%) 0.6164. k=345 beats the anchor by Δ=+0.0082 (p=2.5e-5, Holm 7.5e-5); optimum flag on k=345. All padded-subset pairwise differences are ns (Holm p_adj ≥ 0.18).
- **Runtime vs anchor** (`runs/*/plots/pca_sweep_runtime_t.csv`, Nadeau-Bengio-corrected paired t on 30 fold-seconds): ESM-C — k=96 faster (t=-88.0, p=2.6e-8) and k=9854 slower (t=71.8, p=7.1e-8) as expected, but k=3663 is *faster* than the k=5883 anchor (t=-16.4, p=1.1e-4) and k=2278 *slower* (t=6.49, p=0.0072); wall times are likewise non-monotonic (2278: 14.48 h vs 3663: 9.56 h wall). ESM-IF runtime is monotonic (213.5 s → 4080.1 s mean fold-seconds). `maxrss=NA` throughout (JobAcctGather disabled, per script docstring).
- **Context**: both sweep optima (0.6973 / 0.6411) remain below the committed handcrafted_sparse baseline 0.7409 (`results/tables/padmode_base_metrics.tsv`). The ESM-C 90%-EV anchor value here (0.6885 pooled, k=5883) matches the 300M arm of the previous report's 300M-vs-600M comparison.
