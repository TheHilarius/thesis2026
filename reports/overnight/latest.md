## Code Review — Shortcomings & Issues

13 commits in the range, all reviewed via `git show`. Redundant / over-engineered / reinvented-helper findings first, as prioritised. Severity legend: bug | risk | maintainability | style.

1. **Two implementations of Cliff's delta, and the repo kept the OOM-prone one (maintainability — redundant / reinvented helper)** — `src/pipeline/r/functions.R:1196-1218` computes delta as a chunked `outer()` product over up to 50,000 × 50,000 pairs, while the new `bootstrap_delta()` at `src/pipeline/r/04_evaluate_netmhcpan_sensitivity.R:878-889` computes the *same statistic* with the rank identity (`U = sum(rank(c(x,y))[1..n1]) − n1(n1+1)/2`, `delta = 2U/(n1n2) − 1`) in O(n log n), no chunking and no matrix. The two agree on ties (outer counts a tie as 0 toward both sides of a denominator that includes it; the rank form counts 0.5 — algebraically identical), and they already agree numerically in the tracked CSV (−0.6991 from `cliff_delta_sampled` vs bootstrap CI −0.7039..−0.6929). Why it matters: 7cd9697 had to shrink the chunk from 5000 to 1000 with a "2 GB → OOM risk" comment — a symptom of the wrong implementation, fixed by reducing throughput 5× instead of deleting the matrix. One rank-based `cliff_delta_sampled` body serves all five call sites (2 in this script, 3 in `09_numeric_feature_analysis.R`, plus the A01:01 copy) and removes the ceiling comment entirely.
