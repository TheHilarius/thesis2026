## Experiments / Results

All figures below are read from committed CSVs at origin/hilarius; the underlying `cv_results_*.json` files remain uncommitted (Potential Issues 1). The four shared DeLong families (`all_pairs`, `vs_anchor`, `vs_best`, `subset_*`) plus six ROI families are Holm-adjusted within family.

- **ESM-C-300M pad-token, 11-k ladder** (`runs/pca_sweep_delong/pca_sweep_results.csv`): pooled AUC rises monotonically 0.5563 (k=7, 30 % EV) → 0.6119 (29) → 0.6442 (96) → 0.6735 (291) → 0.6857 (507) → 0.6896 (856) → 0.6949 (1403) → peak 0.6973 at k=2278 (80 % EV), then declines 0.6943 (3663), 0.6885 (5883, 90 %-EV anchor), 0.6835 (9854). In the `vs_best` family every other k is significant after Holm, including k=1403 (Δ=−0.0024, p_adj=0.044 — marginal) and k=3663 (p_adj=0.024), so the optimum-PC rule (smallest k Holm-ns vs best) flags k=2278 = best. k=2278 beats the 90 %-EV anchor by Δ=+0.0087 (z=6.70, Holm p_adj=1.2e-10). k=17909 (99 % EV) is pending (ghost slot in figures, zero-byte log).
- **ESM-IF eos/bos-repeat, 11-k ladder + external anchor**: 0.5878 (k=47, 30 % EV) → 0.6294 (176) → 0.6410 (345) → 0.6448 (632, 60 % EV) → peak 0.6451 at k=850 (65 % EV) → 0.6441 (1143) → 0.6408 (1548) → 0.6402 (2125) → 0.6374 (2974) → 0.6267 (6700) → 0.6164 (11243, 99 %); anchor k=4308 (90 % EV) 0.6328. The previous report's best-at-k=345 (0.6411) is superseded: k=345 is now significantly below k=850 (p_adj=0.036). k=632, 850 and 1143 are mutually indistinguishable (p_adj=0.97) and the optimum rule flags k=632. All three beat the 90 %-EV anchor by ≈ +0.012 (k=850: Δ=+0.0122, p_adj=5.5e-11; k=632: +0.0120, p_adj=5.1e-10; k=1143: +0.0113, p_adj=5.2e-10), as do the 75–85 % EV points at smaller margins.
- **Informative padded subset** (`subset_vs_anchor`): no ESM-IF ladder k beats the anchor subset AUC (best Δ=+0.0051 at k=1143, p_adj=1); for ESM-C, k=2278 vs the anchor is Δ=+0.0145 but ns after Holm (p_adj=0.217, family now 11 pairs). Subset pooled peaks sit at different k than the full-data peaks (ESM-C k=2278: 0.7055; ESM-IF k=1143: 0.6738).
- **Runtime** (`runs/*/plots/pca_sweep_runtime_t.csv`, Nadeau-Bengio paired t on fold-seconds vs the best-AUC k): the carried non-monotonicity persists and is now visible against the new reference — ESM-C k=3663 (1105.9 s mean fold-seconds) is *faster* than the best k=2278 (1684.6 s; t=−21.8, p=2.7e-5), as is k=5883 (t=−6.49, p=0.0072); ESM-IF k=850 (357.8 s) is faster than k=632 (445.9 s), and k=2125 (689.7 s) faster than k=1548 (755.1 s). `maxrss` is NA in all rows (JobAcctGather=none) — the new encodings-sweep sidecar is the first fix for that class.
